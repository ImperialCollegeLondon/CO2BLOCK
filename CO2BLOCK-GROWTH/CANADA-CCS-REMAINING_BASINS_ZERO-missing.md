## Key Takeaways for CO2BLOCK

1. **Only 1 of 35 basins is in Global.xlsx** — representing a 97% gap in database coverage for Canadian basins with zero CCS projects.

2. **None of these basins have operational CCS** — this is consistent with the Canadian CCS reality: all operational projects are concentrated in Alberta (Alberta R&D), Alberta (CorteX), Williston (BD3, Aquistore, Weyburn, Midale), and Suncor (Firebag).

3. **Sverdrup Basin is the most significant omission** among the 34 — it is the only one with a published high-case storage capacity estimate (50 Gt, NRCan/CAPP/MNR 1995) and has active oil spills into the CCS conversation via Polar Kraken.

4. **Queen Charlotte Basin and Lancaster Sound** are the offshore basins with the most CCS literature (TGS/CNSOPB 2016, IGI 2022 marine CO₂ storage studies) — both entirely missing.

5. **The North Slope (UBI 467)** IS correctly present in Global.xlsx with countries USA, Russia, Canada. The Canadian portion (Yukon North Slope / Ivvavik area) is a fringe extension and has zero CCS projects.
