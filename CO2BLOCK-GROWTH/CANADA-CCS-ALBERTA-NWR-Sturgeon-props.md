# NWR Sturgeon Refinery CO₂ Capture — Project Context

**Last updated**: 2026-07-23  
**Project**: NWR Sturgeon Refinery CO₂ Capture (North West Redwater Partnership) — operational 2018+  
**Storage**: CO₂ transported via ACTL pipeline → injected at Clive CO₂-EOR (Leduc D-3A pool)  
**Location**: Sturgeon County, ~45 km northeast of Edmonton, Alberta, Canada  
**Basin**: Alberta Basin / Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin (WCSB)

---

## Project Type

This is a **CO₂ capture facility**, not an injection site. The storage formation properties for the injected CO₂ are documented in `CANADA-CCS-ALBERTA-ACTL-Clive-props.md` (Leduc Formation D-3A pool at Clive, operated by Enhance Energy).

NWR Sturgeon Refinery is the anchor CO₂ supplier for the ACTL system. All captured CO₂ is transported via the 240 km ACTL pipeline and injected at the Clive field for enhanced oil recovery (EOR) and permanent storage.

## Capture Process Detail

1. **Gasification**: Heavy bottoms from bitumen refining are fed into the MPG (Multipurpose Gasification) unit, producing raw syngas.
2. **Shift conversion**: Raw syngas is converted to increase H₂ content.
3. **Rectisol® absorption**: Refrigerated methanol physically absorbs CO₂ from the syngas stream. H₂ exits as the main product; high-purity CO₂ is recovered during solvent regeneration.
4. **Compression**: The pure, dry CO₂ stream is compressed through a booster compressor (within NWR facility) then a main compressor (at WCS Sturgeon Compressor Station, 3.2 km from refinery) to ACTL pipeline pressure.
5. **Transport**: CO₂ enters the 240 km ACTL pipeline for delivery to Clive.
6. **Injection**: Enhance Energy injects CO₂ into the Leduc D-3A pool for miscible EOR and permanent storage.

## Verification

- Capture-to-storage chain: NWR Sturgeon → ACTL pipeline (Wolf Midstream) → Clive injection (Enhance Energy)
- Storage formation: Leduc D-3A pool at Clive (see `CANADA-CCS-ALBERTA-ACTL-Clive-props.md` for reservoir properties)
- All CO₂ from NWR is transported via ACTL; no dedicated on-site storage at the refinery
- Gasification + Rectisol® capture is a pre-combustion process, producing a high-purity CO₂ stream without requiring additional dehydration

---

## Notes

- NWR Sturgeon Refinery is the **world's first refinery designed from the ground up with integrated carbon capture**.
- The refinery reduces CO₂ production by ~70% compared to traditional bitumen upgrading, due to gasification technology.
- Multiple sources cite Phase 1 capture capacity as 1.2 Mtpa; the PCL-built CO₂ recovery system is designed for 1.3 Mtpa transmission. A 2025 source (Incorrys) reports 1.6 Mtpa, which may reflect operational optimization or Phase 2 expansion.
- The ACTL pipeline (14.6 Mtpa capacity) is designed for open-access third-party use and currently operates well below capacity.
- Diesel produced at NWR Sturgeon is the lowest "wells-to-wheels" CO₂ transportation fuel based on heavy feedstock.
