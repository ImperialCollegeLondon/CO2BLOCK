## Assessment Details

### 1. INRS St. Lawrence Lowlands Pre-Feasibility (2010–2014)

| Attribute | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Lead** | INRS-ETE (Institut national de la recherche scientifique — Eau Terre Environnement), Chair in Geological CO₂ Sequestration |
| **Funding** | NRCan (ecoETI/CCS Fund?), MERN, industry partners |
| **Region** | St. Lawrence Lowlands platform, ~18,000 km² (Montreal–Quebec City corridor) |
| **Structural Domains** | (1) **Platform**: flat-lying Cambro-Ordovician succession<br>(2) **Appalachian thrust belt**: imbricated thrust slices of platform rocks |
| **Reservoirs (Platform)** | **Potsdam Group** (Upper Cambrian sandstone): 750 m thick, 6–12% φ, 10–200 mD<br>**Beekmantown Group** (Lower Ordovician dolomite): 450 m, 3–18% φ, 45–250 mD<br>**Trenton/Black River/Chazy Groups** (Middle Ordovician limestone): 375 m, 3–10% φ, 1–300 mD |
| **Reservoirs (Thrust Belt)** | Gas reservoirs in imbricated thrust slices (e.g., Saint-Flavien field) — same formations, structurally trapped |
| **Caprocks** | **Utica Shale** (Middle-Upper Ordovician, >100 m, regional)<br>**Lorraine Group** (siltstone/shale) |
| **Depth** | 1,000–2,500 m (platform); thrust sheets at similar depths |
| **Emitters** | Major industrial CO₂ sources (refineries, cement, steel, power) directly in Lowlands |
| **Methodology** | Bachu et al. (2007) basin-scale effective capacity; site-scale matched capacity |
| **Status** | **Pre-feasibility research** — no regulatory application, no pore space lease, no proponent |
| **Key Publications** | Bédard et al. (2013) *Energy Procedia* 37:5093-5100; GeoConvention 2010 Abstract 0289; INRS final reports |

## Regulatory Framework — Quebec

| Instrument | Scope | CCS Status |
|------------|-------|------------|
| **MERN** (Ministère de l'Énergie et des Ressources naturelles) | *Mining Act* / *Petroleum Resources Act* | **No CCS Act**; no pore space tenure; no long-term liability regime |
| **MELCCFP** (Environnement) | *Environment Quality Act* | Capture facility permitting only |
| **Quebec Sustainable Development Act** | Policy | CCS mentioned as option; no regulatory framework |
| **Federal** | *Impact Assessment Act* | Applies to future projects |

**Critical Gap**: Quebec has **no CCS pore space tenure regime**, **no long-term liability framework**, **no CCS-specific regulator**. MERN regulates petroleum/minerals only.
