# Pathways Alliance CCS Hub — Reservoir & Project Properties

> **Project:** Pathways Alliance CO₂ Transportation Network and Storage Hub
> **Location:** Cold Lake area, northeastern Alberta, Canada
> **Basin:** Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin (WCSB) / Alberta Basin
> **Primary Storage Formation:** Basal Cambrian Sandstone (BCS) — Cambrian-Ordovician saline aquifer
> **Secondary Storage Targets:** McMurray Formation / Clearwater Formation (Lower Cretaceous, Mannville Group) — local
> **Status:** Regulatory Application Filed (Q1 2024); IAAC Registry #89090; Timeline suspended Dec 2024 per proponent request
> **Operators:** Canadian Natural (lead), Cenovus Energy, ConocoPhillips Canada, Imperial Oil, Suncor Energy (MEG Energy was founding member; current proponent list on AER filings = 5)
> **FID:** Not yet reached; contingent on fiscal/policy framework
> **Target Operations Start:** 2029–2030

## 4. Permeability

### Basal Cambrian Sandstone

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Permeability control | "Interstitial clay and silt were found to be a critical control on permeability" | AGS PRS 2024-001 |
| Clay sensitivity | "Even a 2–3% increase in clay content has been found to reduce vertical and horizontal permeability by three orders of magnitude, even in coarse-grained sediments" | AGS PRS 2024-001 |
| Permeability range | Highly variable; no site-specific public data for Cold Lake hub | AGS PRS 2024-001; no Pathways-specific data released |
| Diagenetic impacts | "Potassium feldspar alteration to kaolinite during burial reduces the porosity and permeability" | AGS PRS 2023-001 |

> **Data Quality Note:** The dramatic permeability reduction from minor clay content increase (2–3% → 3 orders of magnitude reduction) makes site-specific permeability characterization critical for injectivity assessment. Pathways has drilled test wells (2024) to evaluate this at the Cold Lake hub location.

## 7. Notes on Data Quality

1. **Regulatory / Pre-FID Stage:** The Pathways Alliance CCS Hub is in pre-FID / regulatory application stage. Detailed technical data (injectivity, well counts, precise storage capacity) is proprietary and has not been publicly filed. The project is one of the world's largest proposed CCS developments but has not yet received final investment decision.

2. **Storage Formation Data:** The primary target (Basal Cambrian Sandstone) is well-characterized regionally by the Alberta Geological Survey (Herbers et al., 2023–2024) and CDL (2023). However, site-specific properties at the Cold Lake hub location are not public. Pathways has drilled 2 test wells (2024) to gather site-specific data.

3. **Source Attribution Philosophy:** All values above include verbatim quotes or specific numerical claims from named sources with URLs. When a value is inferred from adjacent data points (e.g., Quest analogue), this is explicitly noted.

4. **Key Knowledge Gaps (not public):**
   - Exact storage hub boundary / pore space tenure area
   - Number and design of injection wells
   - Site-specific porosity, permeability, net pay at Cold Lake hub
   - Injection pressure / temperature design basis
   - Specific CO₂ capture technology selection for each facility
   - Final cost allocation between government and industry (under negotiation as of July 2026 MOU)

5. **Best Available Regional BCS Data Sources:**
   - AGS PRS 2024-001: Characterization of a Saline Aquifer Unit as a Prime Candidate for CCS: Case Study of the Basal Cambrian Sandstone, Canada (Herbers, Hauck, Gordon — Jan 2024)
   - AGS PRS 2023-001: Depositional Environments, Stratigraphic Packages, and Petrography of the Basal Cambrian Sandstone (Herbers, Hauck, Gordon — Jan 2023)
   - CDL (2023): Regional Geological CCS Assessment of the Basal Cambrian Sandstone — March 2023 (proprietary; confidential until March 2028)

6. **Project Status Change (2025–2026):**
   - June 2024: Pathways Alliance temporarily shut down website due to Competition Act amendments
   - December 2024: IAAC evaluation timeline suspended at proponent request
   - Early 2025: Website restored as "Oil Sands Alliance" (rebranded from Pathways Alliance)
   - July 13, 2026: Tripartite MOU signed between Alberta, Ottawa, and 5 producer companies
