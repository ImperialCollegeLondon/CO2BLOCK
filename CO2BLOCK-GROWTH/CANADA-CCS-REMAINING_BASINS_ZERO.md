## Common Reasons for Zero Activity (All Remaining Basins)

| Barrier | Applicability |
|---------|---------------|
| **No CO₂ emission sources** | All Arctic basins; most interior basins |
| **No CCS regulatory framework** | Federal (CER) has no CCS regulation; NWT/Nunavut/Yukon have no CCS Acts; BC has no CCS Act |
| **No pore space tenure regime** | Only AB, SK have dedicated CCS tenure legislation |
| **No infrastructure** | No CO₂ pipelines, capture facilities, or injection wells |
| **No CCUS funding** | NRCan CCS Fund/Clean Energy Fund: zero awards for these basins |
| **Indigenous governance complexity** | Inuit land claims (Nunavut), Treaty 8/11 (NWT), Yukon First Nations — no CCS precedent |
| **Geological uncertainty** | Sparse well control, limited seismic, no production history (no depleted fields) |
| **Economic non-viability** | Remote, high cost, no anchor tenant, carbon price insufficient |
