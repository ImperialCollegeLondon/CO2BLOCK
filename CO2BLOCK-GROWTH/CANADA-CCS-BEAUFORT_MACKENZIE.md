## Assessment Details

### 1. Mallik Gas Hydrate CO₂-CH₄ Exchange (GSC/JOGMEC, 1998–2008)

| Attribute | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Proponents** | Geological Survey of Canada (GSC), Japan Oil, Gas and Metals National Corporation (JOGMEC), NRCan |
| **Location** | Mallik field, Mackenzie Delta, NWT (69.5°N, 134.5°W) |
| **Concept** | Inject CO₂ into methane hydrate-bearing sediments → CO₂ forms hydrate, releases CH₄ (guest exchange) |
| **Field Tests** | 2002: Thermal stimulation production test<br>2007/2008: Depressurization production tests (JOGMEC/NRCan/Aurora) |
| **CO₂ Injection** | Lab-scale and numerical modeling only; no field CO₂ injection conducted |
| **Basin Verification** | Mallik = Mackenzie Delta = Beaufort-Mackenzie Basin (Evenick UBI 70) |
| **Status** | **Research pilot only** — no commercial CCS project; hydrate-CO₂ exchange remains experimental |
| **Citations** | Dallimore & Collett (2005) *Scientific Results Mallik 2002*; Kurihara et al. (2008) *JOGMEC Mallik 2007/2008*; GSC Open Files 220702, 210723 |
