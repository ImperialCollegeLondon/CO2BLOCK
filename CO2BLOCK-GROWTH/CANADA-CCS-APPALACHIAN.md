## Assessment Details

### 1. St. Lawrence Lowlands CO₂ Storage (INRS / MERN, 2010–2014)

| Attribute | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Lead** | INRS-ETE (Institut national de la recherche scientifique) — Chair in Geological CO₂ Sequestration |
| **Region** | St. Lawrence Lowlands platform (south of Quebec City to Montreal), ~18,000 km² |
| **Structural Domains** | (1) Platform (flat-lying Cambro-Ordovician); (2) Appalachian fold-thrust belt (imbricated thrust slices) |
| **Reservoirs** | **Potsdam Group** (sandstone, 750 m, 6–12% φ, 10–200 mD)<br>**Beekmantown Group** (dolomite, 450 m, 3–18% φ, 45–250 mD)<br>**Trenton/Black River/Chazy** (limestone, 375 m, 3–10% φ, 1–300 mD) |
| **Caprocks** | **Utica Shale** (Ordovician, >100 m, regional); **Lorraine Group** (siltstone/shale) |
| **Depth** | 1,000–2,500 m (platform); thrust sheets at similar depths |
| **Emitters** | Major CO₂ sources (refineries, cement, steel) directly in Lowlands |
| **Capacity Method** | Bachu et al. (2007) basin-scale effective capacity; site-scale matched capacity |
| **Status** | **Pre-feasibility / research** — no regulatory application, no pore space lease |
| **Citations** | Bédard et al. (2013) *Energy Procedia* 37:5093-5100; GeoConvention 2010 abstract 0289; INRS reports |

## Regulatory Framework — Quebec / New Brunswick / Nova Scotia

| Jurisdiction | Instrument | CCS Status |
|--------------|------------|------------|
| **Quebec (MERN)** | *Mining Act* / *Petroleum Resources Act* | **No CCS Act**; no pore space tenure; *Sustainable Development Act* policy only |
| **New Brunswick** | *Oil and Natural Gas Act* | **No CCS legislation** |
| **Nova Scotia** | *Onshore Petroleum Atlas* / *Offshore Accord* | **No CCS regulation** (offshore = CNSOPB) |
| **Federal** | *Impact Assessment Act* | Applies to future projects |

**Critical Gap**: No province in Canadian Appalachians has CCS pore space tenure regime or long-term liability framework.
