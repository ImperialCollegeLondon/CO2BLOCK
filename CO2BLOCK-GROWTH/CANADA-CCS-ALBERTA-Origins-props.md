## Notes on Data Quality

- **Project-well-specific data** (porosity 6 %, permeability <10–>100 mD, gross pay 450 ft, depth, temperature, pressure, salinity) come from CarbonStorage.io, which sources AER well-file data and public submissions. These are the best available public values for the Lacombe 4-36-39-25 well.
- **Regional Leduc Formation ranges** are from AGS synthesis (Grokipedia) and SEPM/GSCOPE literature. They provide context but should not be conflated with the specific pool unless directly confirmed.
- **Lease area (2,491,617 acres)** from CarbonStorage.io may represent total Enhance Energy sequestration holdings (ACTL + Origins) rather than the Origins project alone. Confirm with AER lease maps.
- **CO2 plume radius (4 km, max 5.6 km)** is from the AER appeal proceeding (Appeal 1959099, April 2026), which cites modelled plume extent. This is the most authoritative public plume estimate.
- **Permeability range "<10-+100"** on CarbonStorage.io is ambiguous; interpreted as <10 mD to >100 mD. The regional Leduc range of 1–1000 mD confirms a heterogeneous carbonate system.
- **No specific net-to-gross, net pay, or petrophysical log analyses** are publicly available for the 4-36-39-25 well. AER well logs (if accessed via AER Data Store) could refine these.
- **No core analysis or DST data** have been published for this specific well. Values are likely from wireline logs and pressure transient analysis.
- **All AER-based data** (Approval 13463, App 1956215) are official and current as of July 2025.
- **Reservoir temperature, pressure, and salinity** are within typical ranges for the deep Leduc Formation in central Alberta and are consistent with analogue projects (Quest, ACTL/Clive).
- **The project targets a pressure-depleted saline aquifer** — a different reservoir state from the EOR-focused ACTL/Clive CCS (Leduc D-3A pool). The depletion state affects injectivity and storage capacity estimates.
