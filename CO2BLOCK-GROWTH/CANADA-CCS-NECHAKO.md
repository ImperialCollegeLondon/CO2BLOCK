## Tier 3 Assessment — Geoscience BC Central Interior CCS Assessment (Phase 1, 2024)

| Attribute | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Lead** | Geoscience BC / Petrel Robertson Consulting Ltd. (PRCL) / Foresight Canada |
| **Funding** | Geoscience BC consortium (BC Gov't, industry, Indigenous partners) |
| **Scope** | Nechako Basin (~75,000 km²) — Prince George to Houston, Williams Lake to Quesnel |
| **Method** | Desktop study: 12 exploratory wells (1931–1986), limited seismic/gravity, academic research |
| **Target** | Deep saline aquifers (Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous) — no depleted fields (no production history) |
| **Findings** | **Storage not technically recommended**:<br>• Key challenge: injection rates unachievable in low-quality reservoirs<br>• Major uncertainties: reservoir continuity, containment integrity, reservoir quality<br>• 3 areas NW/west of Williams Lake: limited potential with geological risks<br>• 5 areas west/south of Quesnel: recommended for consideration only<br>• New drilling required to de-risk |
| **CO₂ Supply** | Foresight Canada: challenges with scale, transport distances, emissions composition |
| **High-case Resource** | Sum of 3 sub-basins: ~197 Mt (comparable to NEBC Bluesky/Halfway P90) but **high uncertainty** |
| **Conclusion** | "CO₂ storage in Nechako Basin is very unlikely to be economically or practically feasible; further evaluation not recommended" |
| **Alternative** | Carbon mineralization (ultramafic rocks) — CO2Lock Corp. pilot at SAM property (NE flank) |
| **Citations** | Geoscience BC Report 2024-08; PRCL assessment; Foresight Canada ecosystem study; BC Gov't News Jan 2024/Jan 2025 |

## Regulatory Framework — British Columbia

| Instrument | Scope | CCS Status |
|------------|-------|------------|
| **BCER** (British Columbia Energy Regulator) | Oil & gas wells, pipelines, CO₂ injection (acid gas) | **No dedicated CCS regulation**; acid gas disposal under *Oil and Gas Activities Act* |
| **BC Gov't** (Ministry of Energy, Mines & Low Carbon Innovation) | CCS policy, hydrogen, CleanBC | **No CCS Act**; CleanBC targets (0.6 Mt/yr CCS by 2030) |
| **Geoscience BC** | Public geoscience assessments | **No regulatory authority** — research only |
| **Federal** | *Impact Assessment Act* | Applies to major projects |

**Critical Gap**: No CCS pore space tenure regime; no long-term liability framework; BCER experience = acid gas disposal only (~2.7 Mt since 1996).

## Conclusion

**Nechako Basin has ZERO CCS projects.** Geoscience BC Phase 1 assessment (2024) **concludes CO₂ storage is not technically recommended** due to low reservoir quality, injection rate challenges, and major uncertainties. No regulatory framework exists for CCS in BC (BCER = acid gas only). Alternative carbon mineralization (CO2Lock) at pilot concept stage only. Basin assignment confirmed.
