## Barriers / Risks Identified

| Project | Key Barriers |
|---------|--------------|
| **Boundary Dam** | Capture rate variability (85% vs 90% design); no FID on Units 4/5; carbon price uncertainty pre-2019 |
| **Aquistore** | Research-scale (not commercial); limited 3rd-party access; deep (3.2 km) = high drilling cost |
| **Weyburn/Midale** | EOR-dependent economics; **CO₂ supply tied to Dakota Gasification (coal-dependent, faces economic pressure)** — single-source cross-border dependency represents material supply-chain risk; no dedicated saline storage alternative |
| **Dakota Gas Pipeline** | Cross-border regulatory complexity; single-source dependency; no open access |

**Induced seismicity note**: Williston Basin has extensive deep injection history (EOR, saltwater disposal). Deep Deadwood injection at Aquistore (3.2 km, basement-proximal) warrants monitoring for potential induced seismicity, though no events have been attributed to date. SK lacks the regulatory seismicity protocols present in Alberta (AER Directive 080/090). Current Aquistore MMV includes microseismic monitoring.

## Negative Findings (Tier 1–4)

- **No CCS projects in Manitoba portion of Williston Basin** — SME jurisdiction ends at provincial boundary; Manitoba *Captured Carbon Storage Act* (Royal Assent 2024) regulations not yet promulgated as of search date (regulations were expected within 18 months of Royal Assent)
- **No projects in Alberta portion** — Alberta projects map to Alberta Basin (WCSB), not Williston
- **No federal CER-regulated offshore CCS** — Williston is entirely onshore in Canada
- **No DAC projects with Williston storage** — TerraFixing (QC) storage TBD

## Conclusion

**The Canadian Williston Basin hosts 4 operational CCS projects + 1 cross-border pipeline — the largest concentration outside Alberta.** All are in **southern Saskatchewan**, regulated by **SME** under SK-specific CCS legislation. Storage targets: **Mississippian Midale Beds (CO₂-EOR)** and **Cambro-Ordovician Deadwood/Black Island (saline aquifer)**. No projects in Manitoba portion (regulations pending). Basin assignment verified spatially and stratigraphically.
