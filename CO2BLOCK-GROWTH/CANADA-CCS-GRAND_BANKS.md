## Assessment Details

### EGU26-770: Feasibility Assessment of Carbon Sequestration in the Grand Banks (EGU 2026)

| Attribute | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Lead** | Academic collaboration (Dalhousie University of Stavanger, Dalhousie) |
| **Scope** | Grand Banks region offshore Newfoundland — Jeanne d'Arc Basin |
| **Method** | 2D seismic reflection (Petrel Schlumberger) → structural/fault framework → Petex MOVE fault/stress analysis → numerical CO₂ injection simulation |
| **Key Geological Features** | Two dominant normal fault sets (N-S, E-W) coupled with salt structures; faults as barriers/conduits; salt bodies influence trapping efficiency |
| **Target** | Reservoir-caprock pairs in Jeanne d'Arc Basin Mesozoic strata |
| **Constraints** | Tectonic activity, seismic history, structural complexity; fault reactivation risk; induced seismicity thresholds |
| **Status** | **Academic feasibility** — EGU General Assembly 2026 Abstract EGU26-770; no regulatory filing, no proponent |
| **Citation** | EGU26-770 Abstract (meetingorganizer.copernicus.org) |
