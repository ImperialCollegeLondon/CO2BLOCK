- **Line 39** — "Anticosti Basin (Romaine Fm dolomite)" — classified as "Academic speculation." This is accurate. But note that Anticosti has had significant exploration (Corridor Resources/Petrolia wells, 3D seismic) that provides geological data even if CCS was not pursued.
- **Line 26** — "NO OPERATIONAL / CONSTRUCTION / FID PROJECTS" — correct for Canada, but consider adding "Note: US Appalachian Basin has active CarbonSAFE projects" as a parenthetical.
- **No mention of the **Bécancour industrial zone** — Bécancour (QC) has significant CO₂ sources (refinery, methanol, fertilizers) and is geologically connected to the Lowlands/Appalachian transition. It could be a future CCS hub location.
