## Strengths

1. **Cross-border infrastructure correctly handled** — Dakota Gasification pipeline (320 km from ND to SK) is correctly classified as transport infrastructure with its own regulatory citations (NEB/CER, SME).

2. **Clear distinction between EOR and dedicated saline storage** — Weyburn/Midale (EOR) vs. Aquistore (dedicated saline) are correctly distinguished. The BD3 document correctly shows both pathways.

3. **Pore space tenure regime correctly documented** — Saskatchewan's *Captured Carbon Storage Act* (2020) is distinguished from Alberta's MMA regime. This is important cross-jurisdictional context.

4. **Cumulative storage figures** — >5 Mt captured (BD3), ~30 Mt at Weyburn, ~10 Mt at Midale, ~0.5 Mt at Aquistore. These are valuable for basin-scale accounting.

5. **Manitoba gap explicitly flagged** — the negative findings section correctly notes that Manitoba has no CCS projects and its 2024 *Captured Carbon Storage Act* is "not yet implemented."

6. **Geological storage assessments** — PCOR Partnership Atlas, Basal Cambrian, Winnipeg Formation, Mississippian carbonates are all well-referenced with formation-specific capacity estimates.

## Issues & Recommendations

### 1. Aquistore Classification

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Aquistore is listed as "Operational (2013+)" | Aquistore began injection testing in 2013 but was operated as a research/demonstration project, not a commercial sequestration scheme. It has no AER-style Directive 065 approval — it operates under an SME research permit. |
| **Recommendation** | Consider reclassifying Aquistore as "Operational (Research/Demo)" or adding a note clarifying its research permit status vs. commercial operational status. |

### 2. Missing Manitoba Regulatory Update

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Manitoba *Captured Carbon Storage Act* (2024) | Noted as "not yet implemented" — but the audit date is 2025-07-23. By this date there may have been regulatory progress (regulations filed, pore space lease framework published). |
| **Recommendation** | Check Manitoba's CCS regulatory status as of the search date and update if regulations have been promulgated. |

### 3. Cumulative Injection Data Timeliness

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Weyburn: "~30 Mt CO₂ stored to date" | Document date is 2025-07-23 — confirm this is current through 2024/2025 data (Whitecap Resources annual reports, CER Table). |
| Aquistore: "Contingent resource ~300 Mt" | This is a resource estimate, not cumulative injection. The distinction should be clearer in the findings summary table. |
| **Recommendation** | Add an "As of" column or footnote to injection totals. Distinguish "stored to date" vs. "contingent resource" in the findings table. |

### 4. Cross-Border Regulatory Complexity

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Dakota Gas pipeline | Correctly identified as cross-border (NEB/CER), but the document doesn't discuss the implications if Dakota Gasification ceases operations (the plant is coal-dependent and faces economics pressure). |
| **Recommendation** | Add a risk note about single-source CO₂ dependency for Weyburn/Midale EOR operations. |

### 5. Weyburn MMV Citation Gap

| Issue | Detail |

### 6. Missing Reference Detail

| Line | Item | Suggestion |

### 7. Minor Issues

- **Line 111**: "P10: 187 Mt; P50: 360 Mt; P90: 629 Mt" — These are inverted. P10 should be the largest value and P90 the smallest. Check the source ordering.
- **Line 50**: References "IEA GHG Weyburn reports (2000-2012)" — the WHITE CAP project actually extended monitoring to 2016+ through PTRC. Consider adding the post-2012 monitoring references.
- **No induced seismicity discussion** — Williston Basin has extensive injection history (EOR, saltwater disposal). A brief note on induced seismicity risk would strengthen the document (especially for deep Deadwood injection at Aquistore at 3.2 km).
