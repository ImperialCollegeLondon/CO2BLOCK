## Conclusion

**Alberta hosts 8 major CCS projects at various stages — all within the Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin (Evenick Alberta Basin, UBI 13).** Regulatory primacy rests with the AER under Directive 065 and the *Mines and Minerals Act* pore space tenure regime. Three projects are **operational** (Quest, ACTL/Clive, NWR Capture), one **under construction** (Polaris/Atlas), two **AER-approved** (Wolf Lamont, Origins), one **in evaluation/sequestration agreement** (Wabamun), and one **in federal/provincial regulatory application** (Pathways). Total operational storage: **~10+ Mt stored to date**; planned capacity: **>50 Mtpa by 2030**.

No CCS projects exist in Alberta outside the WCSB/Alberta Basin. All basin assignments verified spatially and stratigraphically against primary regulatory coordinates.
